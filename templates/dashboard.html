Why Campaigns May Fail Meta Rules

  • 24-Hour Care Window: Meta forbids free-form text to contacts who haven't messaged you in 24 hours. Only recent contacts receive regular broadcasts.
  • Sandbox Restriction: In Meta Dev Mode, messages only deliver to verified numbers in your Meta Developer Console.
  • Phone Format: Use international E.164 (e.g. 2348012345678) without + or leading 0.
